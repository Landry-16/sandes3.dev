At the top of the page, an author's messy desk: index cards, sticky notes, a map, a manuscript. As you scroll, everything gets put away, down to the blank sheet of the beta sign-up. Each section lets you try a real feature of the product.

## Details

- All the copy lives in a single JSON file, and French typography is applied at render time.
- Figma mock-ups for desktop, mobile and the animation storyboard.
- End-to-end tests with Playwright and unit tests with Vitest.
