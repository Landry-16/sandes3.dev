Built for hospitals, whose teams manage Active Directory accounts and groups with the ADUC console, which is slow and poorly suited to bulk operations. Power AD offers a modern interface to search, compare and manage groups.

## Architecture

- A modular PowerShell engine: configuration, logging, Active Directory abstraction, data validation.
- A Material Design WPF interface, with comparison and settings windows.
- Group comparison between users to spot missing rights quickly.

Ongoing project, private repository.
