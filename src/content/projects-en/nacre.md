Nacre is the name of my freelance business: clear, fast showcase websites for independent consultants and service providers. The site has to sell without a pitch, by showing the work and listing its prices.

It opens on a film of living nacre, the inside of a pearl oyster filmed in macro. As you scroll, the demo of Maison Pétrin, a fictional bakery, fills the screen and replays the making of its website: a pencil draws the sketch, the mock-up settles, the colours arrive, the site goes live, then its opening hours are updated.

## What it does

- The film of the method, driven by scrolling, while the steps run in the left column.
- Six demo templates, fictional shops, on an endless necklace, each with a live demo.
- A public price grid kept in a single file, which feeds the pricing page, the sample invoice and the structured data.
- Contact and free audit forms, with a honeypot field and sending states.
- Two themes, white nacre and black nacre, following the visitor's setting.
- A French version and an English version.

## Details

- Fully static site, with no cookies or trackers.
- Opening film generated once in 4K, then slowed down and edited into a seamless loop of about 70 seconds.
- Screenshots and videos of the templates recorded automatically from the demos.
- Pages and forms tested end to end, on desktop and phone.
